These are real photographs and one still from a real cooking video. They are visual references for the dishes, not photographs of the exact recipes prepared by the application owner. Ingredients, garnishes and presentation may differ. Specific differences are listed below and should stay visible in recipe credits.

The Pexels photograph was taken on May 3, 2022 with an iPhone 8. The brigadeiro image is a video still at 02:18, not a generated illustration.
